1. QUICK START
========================================================================
1. Double-click "PureClip.exe" (or launch PureClip from your Start Menu).
2. Drag and drop any video or audio file into the application window,
   or click "Browse File".
3. Choose your desired output format (MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, MP3, AAC, WAV, GIF).
4. Select an optimization preset:
   • Stream Copy  : Lossless instant container swap (< 1 second)
   • Balanced     : Optimal quality & 60-75% file size reduction (Recommended)
   • High Quality : Pristine visual fidelity
   • Compact      : Ultra-small size for Discord, messaging & email
5. Click "Start Conversion".
6. When complete, click "Reveal in File Explorer" to view your converted file.

========================================================================
2. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
========================================================================

Q1: Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" on first launch.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A: This happens on Windows for any newly compiled, open-source application
   downloaded from the internet that has not yet built cloud download reputation.
   PureClip is 100% open-source, contains NO malware or telemetry, and bundles
   an official FFmpeg binary.
   
   To launch:
   1. Click "More info".
   2. Click "Run anyway".
   Windows will remember your decision and will not ask again.

Q2: Do I need to install Python or FFmpeg separately?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A: No! PureClip is completely self-contained. The official FFmpeg 7.1 binary
   and high-speed conversion engine are pre-bundled in the app package.

Q3: Does PureClip upload my files to the cloud?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A: Never. All conversion and metadata scrubbing happens 100% locally on your
   own device. Your private videos never leave your computer.

Q4: What metadata does the "Privacy Clean" feature remove?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A: By default, PureClip strips hidden camera device serial numbers, GPS
   location tags, creation timestamps, and recording software telemetry
   from the output media.
